RESKATE VR - QUEST 2 CAMERA / LAUNCHER v3 (EXPERIMENTAL)

INSTALL
1. Close skate. and ReSkateLauncher. Back up the files from your current build.
2. Extract this ZIP and drag its files into your existing ReSkate folder,
   beside Skate.exe and ReSkateLauncher.exe. Replace files when asked.
3. Connect Meta Quest Link / Air Link. Open ReSkateLauncher.exe and click LAUNCH VR.
4. For normal desktop play, click PLAY instead. This disables VR for that session.
5. Enter gameplay, sit/stand comfortably with your head straight, then press F8.

No PowerShell, Python, compiler or scripts are needed to play.

WHAT CHANGED
- Separate PLAY and LAUNCH VR buttons; standard play keeps your saved display options.
- Push animations no longer supply the player rig heading; it follows the trajectory root.
- Tick and render camera updates use the same raw head pose and player frame in VR.
- ADDONS > VR > Show game HUD toggles scores, objectives and indicators.
- The camera starts at the avatar's eye origin instead of behind and to one side.
- A rigid player eye origin replaces following animated head bob and neck turns.
- Physical headset rotation and leaning are applied directly, without animation filters.
- Extra future head-pose prediction is removed; image pairing is kept as you set it.
- Automatic flip/bail/grab camera changes are off in the starting camera setup.
- The update keeps your existing depth, FOV calibration and eye-pairing settings.

New camera defaults are applied once automatically after your profile loads.
Later changes are saved normally. Head-centred tracking is a toggle in ADDONS > VR.
F8 resamples the avatar eye origin as well as recentering headset tracking.

SMOOTHNESS / DISPLAY
This build changes tracking behavior, not the game's rendering architecture.
If the game cannot reach the headset frame rate, head turns can still stutter.
For the next test, use 72 Hz in Link, game frame limit Unlimited, V-Sync Off,
frame generation Off and motion blur Off. Lower game graphics until it holds
that rate. In the GAME's display menu, select Windowed and 1440 x 1440 as a
starting point; the game can replace the launcher's requested square resolution
when it loads saved display settings. Raise resolution once motion is stable.

TEST STATUS
Native rotation/lean, push-heading, preset-migration and launch-mode checks pass.
The Windows DLL and launcher compile; all three stereo shaders compile.
This revision has not yet been tested on a physical headset.
The HUD is the game HUD included in the reconstructed image; edge placement
and depth can retain reconstruction artifacts. It is not a separate floating panel. If turning,
pitch or roll still causes stretching or double vision, stop and report it.
Do not force yourself to keep testing an uncomfortable image.

This remains stereo reconstructed from one view. It cannot yet promise the
image stability, latency or quality of a game built for native VR.
To roll back, close the game and restore the previous DLL and launcher.
Auto-update and built-in Spotify Connect remain disabled for this test build.
