RESKATE VR - QUEST 2 TEST BUILD

INSTALL
1. Close skate. and ReSkateLauncher. Back up your ReSkate folder.
2. Extract this ZIP and drag its files into your existing ReSkate folder,
   beside Skate.exe and ReSkateLauncher.exe. Replace files when asked.
3. Connect your Quest 2 through Meta Quest Link / Air Link.
4. Open ReSkateLauncher.exe and click LAUNCH VR.

No PowerShell, Python, compiler, or scripts are needed to play.
The launcher enables VR, starts in a 2048 x 2048 window, and applies the
Quest 2 balanced settings the first time. Your later VR adjustments are saved.
The menu appears on a screen in the headset; the tracked VR camera takes
over during gameplay. Press F8 to recenter after entering the game.

This is an add-on for an existing, working ReSkate installation. It does not
include skate., game data, or Steam files. OpenXR must already be working
through your Link / Air Link setup.

TEST STATUS
Windows binaries, native math tests, and embedded shaders passed their checks.
The eye-projection changes still need testing on a physical Quest 2.
Game-version compatibility has not been verified on your installation.
If you still see double vision, stop the test and report whether it is
horizontal, vertical, or changes as you turn your head. You can restore the
backed-up files with the game closed. Do not force yourself to keep testing
an image that is uncomfortable.

This build preserves the original depth-search quality. It reconstructs
stereo from one rendered view, so some edge artifacts remain possible.
Built-in Spotify Connect is omitted. Binary auto-update is disabled for this
test build. Matching source and licenses accompany this build.
